## File Map

- **REFERENCE.md** — type-specific rules (emails, blog posts, social media, support content) plus grammar &
  mechanics. Read the section matching the content type you're working on.
- **EXAMPLES.md** — before/after rewrites for each content type, with the rule applied called out. Use these as
  few-shot patterns for how a rewrite should look and read.

## Workflow

1. **Identify the content type** — email (subject or body), blog heading, social post (LinkedIn/Twitter/X), or
   support reply.
2. **Apply the Core Principles** below to produce a first-pass rewrite.
3. **Consult REFERENCE.md** for the rules specific to that content type, plus Grammar & Mechanics.
4. **Consult EXAMPLES.md** for a matching before/after pair and mirror that pattern.
5. **Return the polished text**, followed by a brief **Changelog:** — a short bullet list of what changed and why.

## Core Principles

- **Voice**: warm, direct, confident — like a knowledgeable colleague, not a corporation.
- **Tone**: adapts by context — transactional (neutral, efficient), marketing (enthusiastic, benefit-led, never
  hypey), support (empathy-first, then solution-first).
- **Length preference**: short sentences, short paragraphs, no filler.
- **Person**: "you" for the reader, "we" for the company — never third person ("the user," "customers").

## Instant Rules

Quick checklist to run over any rewrite:

- [ ] No jargon or corporate filler (check the Jargon → Plain English table in EXAMPLES.md).
- [ ] Active voice, not passive.
- [ ] Contractions used where natural (we're, you'll, don't).
- [ ] Numbers: spelled out zero–nine, digits for 10+ (always digits for versions/%/currency/dates).
- [ ] Oxford comma used consistently.
- [ ] At most one exclamation mark in the whole piece; em dashes used sparingly.
