## Email Subject Lines

| ❌ Before | ✅ After | Rule Applied |
|---|---|---|
| Important: Please Read Regarding Your Account Status Update | Your account needs one quick fix | Lead with the action, cut filler |
| Re: Re: FWD: Invoice | Your March invoice is ready | Avoid stacked prefixes and vague labels |
| WE ARE PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE OUR NEW FEATURE!!! | Meet Workspace: your new home base | No ALL CAPS, no exclamation stacking |
| Notification of Service Interruption | Heads up: brief downtime tonight | Plain English over corporate-speak |

## Email Body Rewrites

❌ **Before**
> We are pleased to inform you that your request has been processed successfully by our dedicated support team.

✅ **After**
> Good news — we've taken care of your request.

**Changes**: cut the passive voice and corporate filler ("we are pleased to inform you," "dedicated support team"), led with the outcome, used a contraction, and added a warm, human opener.

## Blog Heading Rewrites

| ❌ Before (label-style) | ✅ After (outcome-focused) |
|---|---|
| Deployment Optimization | Cut your deploy time in half |
| API Rate Limiting | Stop hitting rate limits before they hit you |
| Onboarding Overview | Get your team live in one afternoon |
| Security Considerations | Lock down your workspace in 10 minutes |

## Common Jargon → Plain English

| Jargon | Plain English |
|---|---|
| leverage | use |
| utilize | use |
| circle back | follow up |
| reach out | contact / email |
| synergy | teamwork |
| at this time | right now |
| in order to | to |
| going forward | from now on |
| per our conversation | as we discussed |
| best-in-class | (cut it, or say "great") |

## Social Media Rewrites

**LinkedIn**

❌ **Before**
> We are thrilled to announce the launch of our brand new feature set, which has been meticulously engineered by our world-class team to deliver unparalleled value to our valued customers. #Innovation #Excellence #ProductLaunch #Enterprise

✅ **After**
> We just shipped something that'll save your team hours every week.
>
> Meet Workspace — one place for your docs, tasks, and chats. No more tab-switching.
>
> Try it free today. #ProductLaunch

**Changes**: cut the corporate superlatives ("thrilled," "meticulously engineered," "unparalleled," "world-class"), led with the reader's benefit in the first two lines, trimmed hashtags from four to one.

**Twitter/X**

❌ **Before**
> We would like to take this opportunity to inform our followers that, effective immediately, our platform will be undergoing a series of enhancements. #update #software #tech #news

✅ **After**
> We're rolling out a faster, cleaner dashboard today. Same login, zero setup. 🎉

**Changes**: dropped the throat-clearing opener, put the news in the first sentence, cut hashtags from four to zero, added one sparing emoji instead of stacked tags.

## Support Reply Rewrites

❌ **Before**
> Per our policy, refunds are only processed within 30 days of purchase. Since your request falls outside this window, we are unable to process a refund at this time.

✅ **After**
> Let's see what we can do here. Your purchase is just past our 30-day refund window, so I can't issue a refund directly — but I can offer you a credit toward your next order, or look into other options with my manager. Which would you prefer?

**Changes**: replaced the policy-first, distancing language ("per our policy," "we are unable") with an empathy-first, partnering opener ("Let's see what we can do"), and closed with a concrete alternative instead of a dead end.
